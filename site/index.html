for fans of

skraeckoedlan, elder, mastodon, pallbearer

the weight of doom, the swing of stoner rock, the restlessness of prog. riffs that circle, sink, stop dead and come back bigger. and above them, a voice.

  1. always fail

    recorded, and still under wraps.

  2. downward

    a lone, quiet opening, then the floor gives way. slow and heavy, it sinks into a drone before the last descent.

  3. hatchling

    the restless one. dense and driving, until it stretches out, hangs on one enormous chord and breaks out of the shell.

  4. hivemind

    a riff that circles until it takes over. it stops dead, twice, and returns as a single mass.

  5. reptilian call

    a five-minute summoning: two long cycles of riff and voice, then the whole creature uncoils.

  6. violent sea

    recorded, and still under wraps.

one line

Slowth (FI): prog / stoner / doom, featuring members of Coochilla, Craneium, I, Captain and Malamute.

short

Slowth is a prog, stoner and doom supergroup from Turku, Finland, uniting members of Coochilla, Craneium, I, Captain and Malamute. Their first six songs are being mixed and mastered by Skraeckoedlan’s Robert Lamu, ahead of four singles and a debut album planned for 2027.

long

Slow growth, heavy roots. Slowth brings together four musicians from the heavy underground of Turku, Finland: Jacke (Malamute) on vocals and guitar, Andreas (I, Captain) on guitar, Hector (Coochilla) on bass and Joel (Craneium) on drums. Together they join the weight of doom, the swing of stoner rock and the restlessness of prog, with riffs that circle, sink, stop dead and come back bigger. The band’s first six songs are recorded and are being mixed and mastered by Robert Lamu of Swedish fuzz veterans Skraeckoedlan. Four singles are planned for 2027, leading to a debut album at the end of the year. With four more songs already being written, Slowth is setting its sights on stages across Europe.
