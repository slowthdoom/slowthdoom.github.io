a new band.
old roots.

Slowth is a prog, stoner and doom band from Turku, Finland: Jacke (Malamute) on vocals and guitar, Andreas (I, Captain) on guitar, Hector (Coochilla) on bass and Joel (Craneium) on drums. Between them they have years of records, club shows, festivals and tours with their other bands. Slowth has recorded six songs, which are being mixed and mastered by Robert Lamu of the Swedish band Skraeckoedlan. The band debuts in 2027 with four singles, followed by a six-song album at the end of the year. Four new songs are being written. Martin Ahlö, guitarist of Craneium, is the tour manager. Slowth is looking to play and tour in Europe.
