SLOWTH TECH RIDER

Stage left and stage right are from the performers’ perspective.

Band setup

  • Sound engineer: Slowth brings its own sound engineer.
  • Amps: Slowth always plays through amps and cabs, never impulse responses.
  • Guitars: Both guitar cabs are miked.
  • Bass: DI out of the Darkglass, plus two cabs on stage. The bass cabs are not miked.

Venue-provided equipment and requirements

  • Microphones: Full microphone setup according to the input list below.
  • Microphone stands: All required microphone stands. The band does not provide any stands.
  • Monitors: Four monitors, positioned according to the stage plot.
  • DI boxes: None needed.
  • Cabling: All necessary XLR cables, including one for the bass DI out.
  • Power: Stage power drops for every band member and amp, positioned according to the stage plot.
  • PA system: A professional, full-range sound system suitable for the venue and audience capacity.
